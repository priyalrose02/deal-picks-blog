Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Evelia collects, how it's used, and the third-party services (including advertising and affiliate programs) that operate on this site.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Evelia Home & Fashion (evelia, "we," "us") is an independently run blog operated by Priyal. This Privacy Policy applies to this website and explains how we handle information when you visit.

Information we collect

We do not require account creation or collect sensitive personal information to browse this site. We may collect:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name and email address if you contact us through the Contact page.
  • Usage data — such as pages visited, time on site, browser type, and approximate location, collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device to remember preferences and support the advertising and analytics services described below.

Google AdSense & advertising cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks. These companies use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Partner Sites policy.

Amazon Associates disclosure

Evelia is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases — at no additional cost to you. Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change.

Other affiliate links

In addition to Amazon, some posts may contain affiliate links to other retailers or services. If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely believe are useful.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to external sites, including Amazon and Pinterest, that are not operated by us. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the site. You may also opt out of interest-based advertising through the Google Ads Settings link above or through the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
